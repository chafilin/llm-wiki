# ⚙️ Soil Mixes
- Chamaedorea / Spider plant → 70% soil + 30% perlite
- Calathea → 60% soil + 20% perlite + 20% coco
- Pachira / Strelitzia → 50% soil + 30% perlite + 20% coco

---

# ⚠️ Important
* Don’t choose pots that are too large
* Make sure pots have drainage holes
* Don’t overwater right after repotting
* Keep plants away from radiators
