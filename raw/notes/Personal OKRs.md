# Personal OKRs
## Objective 1 — Improve my delivery velocity and flow
**Key Results (pick 3–5):**
* Increase my completed work throughput by **X%** vs last quarter (use a simple baseline: avg tasks/week or story points/sprint—whatever your team uses).
* Reduce average cycle time (In Progress → Done) for my tasks by **X%**.
* Keep sprint spillover/carryover for my owned commitments below **X%** (e.g., <15–20%).
* Reduce “work in progress” to **≤2 active items at a time** for **≥80%** of the quarter (less context switching = faster finish rate).
* Unblock faster: raise blockers within **24 hours** and keep “blocked time” under **X days per sprint**.

## Objective 2 — Improve my task estimation accuracy and predictability
**Key Results (pick 3–5):**
* **≥70%** of my tasks finish within **±20%** of estimate (or your team’s norm).
* **100%** of tasks > **X days** have written scope, assumptions, risks, and a confidence level (high/med/low).
* For each sprint, keep “planned vs done” variance within **X%** for my committed items.
* Do a quick estimate retro for **N tasks**: estimate vs actual + 1 lesson captured.

## Objective 3 — Improve visibility of my task progress
**Key Results (pick 3–5):**
* **100%** of my active work is tracked in one place with a clear status (In progress / Blocked / Review / Done).
* Post **1–2 updates per week** with: what shipped, what’s next, blockers, ETA changes.
* For items > **X days**, add a progress note at least **2× per week** (next step + owner + date).
* Raise changes early: communicate ETA or scope changes within **24 hours** of discovering them.
