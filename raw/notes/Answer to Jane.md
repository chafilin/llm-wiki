Hi Jane!

Thank you for sending the selection of properties and for your prompt response. We have carefully reviewed all the offers.

Unfortunately, none of the proposed apartments were suitable for us. I have listed the main reasons below so that you can consider our preferences for the next search:

Apartment 1: We were not satisfied with the overall condition and design. The interior and furniture appear outdated and do not meet our expectations.

Apartment 2: The layout and internal finishing suit us, but we have doubts regarding the house/general condition of the building itself. Additionally, the 7-year contract is an unacceptable condition for us at this time.

Apartment 3: We felt that the apartment had overly specific architectural solutions (wall-to-wall windows, very high ceilings). Judging by the photos, the property looks unfinished, uncomfortable, and cold, which makes its assessment and perception very difficult.

Apartment 4: The apartment is very old, with an unclear layout, but the key issue is the extremely inconvenient and non-functional bathroom design, which completely fails to meet our requirements.

To summarize, the critical factors for us are: modern and comfortable design, good condition of the building, and flexible contract terms (not 7 years).
