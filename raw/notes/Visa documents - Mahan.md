# Visa documents - Mahan
#relocation

- [x] National visa application form (attached), 2 copies, duly signed by the person in whose name the visa is to be issued, and two photographs with the following characteristics: recent, 3.5 x 4.5 color, front view, head uncovered and white background. 
- [x] Original passport and a copy of all pages of the passport, including blank pages, with a minimum validity of one year and at least two continuous pages without stamps/visas. 

- [x] The original marriage certificate, issued within the last 3 months and translated into Spanish by a **sworn translator recognized by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs**

- [x] At the time of submitting the application, the established public fee must be paid, which will be the equivalent of 80 euros, as a general rule. Please bring cash because in some cases they do not allow card payment.

- [x] The original (and a copy) criminal record certificate of the countries where you have had legal residence during the last 5 years and issued within the last 90 days prior to the appointment , duly apostilled/legalized and translated into Spanish by a **sworn translator recognized by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs**  (we will help with the translation if needed).

- [x] Spanish Resolution.

- [x] Proof of appointment at the Consulate, printed.
