# Visa documents - My
#relocation

- [x] National visa application form (attached), 2 copies, duly signed by the person in whose name the visa is to be issued, and two photographs with the following characteristics: recent, 3.5 x 4.5 color, front view, head uncovered and white background. 

- [x] Original passport and a copy of all pages of the passport, including blank pages, with a minimum validity of one year and at least two continuous pages without stamps/visas. 

- [x] At the time of submitting the application, the established public fee must be paid, which will be the equivalent of **€80**, as a general rule. For citizens of the United States of America, the fee is **$190** . For nationals of Australia, Bangladesh, Canada, and the United Kingdom different rates are applied for reasons of reciprocity. In these cases, the amount of the fee must be consulted with the Consular Office. Please bring cash because in some cases they do not allow card payment.  

- [x] The original (and a copy) criminal record certificate of the countries where you have had legal residence during the last 2 years and **issued within the last 90 days prior to the appointment**, duly apostilled/legalized and translated into Spanish by a **sworn translator recognized by the Spanish Ministry of Foreign Affairs**  (we will help with the translation if needed).  

- [x] Your Spanish work contract

- [x] Spanish Resolution

- [x] Any proof of residence at your current address (residence card, driver's license, electricity or phone bill in your name....) Sometimes they don't ask for it but since it depends on the consulate and often on the official in charge of receiving your application, I prefer that you have it ready.

- [x] Proof of appointment at the Consulate, printed.
