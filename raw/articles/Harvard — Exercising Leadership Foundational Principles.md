# Exercising Leadership: Foundational Principles

Source: https://pll.harvard.edu/course/exercising-leadership-foundational-principles

**Institution:** Harvard University (Harvard Kennedy School)
**Platform:** edX
**Instructor:** Ronald Heifetz — Founder of the Center for Public Leadership, senior lecturer at Harvard Kennedy School

## Course Overview

This introductory course explores strategies for leading through organizational change and adaptive challenges. Participants examine how to mobilize people to address complex problems while building resilience amid transformation.

## Learning Outcomes

Students will develop competency in:

- Breaking down complicated organizational challenges into manageable components
- Recognizing distinctions between formal and informal authority structures
- Analyzing stakeholder perspectives and interests
- Establishing and strengthening trust-based relationships
- Managing disagreement constructively
- Adopting personal resilience practices for navigating change

## Course Description

The course addresses "adaptive challenges" facing organizations and communities in times of crisis. Instructors guide participants through frameworks for "leading in a changing world" and encourage reflection on personal leadership priorities.
