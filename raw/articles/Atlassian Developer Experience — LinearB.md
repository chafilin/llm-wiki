# Atlassian Developer Experience: A Journey Toward Engineering Excellence

**Source:** https://linearb.io/blog/atlassian-developer-experience-engineering-excellence
**Author:** Ben Lloyd Pearson
**Date:** August 8, 2025

## Starting Point

CTO Rajeev Rajan discovered developer satisfaction was below 50%. Launched a multi-year transformation centered on "developer joy unlocks sustained productivity."

## Core Problems Identified

1. Difficult development processes
2. Perceived lack of autonomy to address known issues
3. Fear of breaking complex systems dependent on tribal knowledge

## Dual-Strategy Framework

### Centralized Champions Program
Cross-functional group representing every engineering team identifies organization-wide investments and drives initiatives (e.g., remote development environments).

### The "10% Principle"
Each engineering team receives explicit budgeted time (10% of capacity) for productivity improvements, tracked in Jira. Teams can invest more if justified.

## Metrics Evolution

From PR cycle time only → to:
- Issue cycle time (Jira ticket start to closure)
- Self-service documentation effectiveness
- Dependency maintenance ease
- Monthly developer satisfaction surveys

## Results

- One team: 48-hour production deployment rate from 12% → 32% in one quarter
- Another team: 300% improvement in shared service deployment frequency

## Tooling: Compass

Atlassian's internal developer portal (later productized):
- Centralized component catalog
- Health scorecards monitoring architecture
- Software templates promoting consistency
- Extensible for team-specific needs

## Key Insight

Success requires balancing top-down executive sponsorship with bottom-up team empowerment and tight feedback loops — making developer experience a core business function.
