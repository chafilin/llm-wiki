# Spotify Backstage — Soundcheck

**Source:** https://backstage.spotify.com/docs/plugins/soundcheck

## What It Is

Soundcheck is a Backstage plugin for measuring and improving software health across an engineering organization. "Soundcheck incentivizes quality, reliability, and alignment of your software ecosystem."

## Core Concepts

1. **Check** — A measurable standard or best practice evaluated against components
2. **Check Result** — The pass/fail outcome when a check runs against a component
3. **Track** — A longer-term technology health improvement initiative
4. **Level** — Groupings of checks representing milestone achievements within a track
5. **Certification** — Recognition earned by passing all checks at a given level

## How It Works

Checks are organized into Tracks with progressive Levels. Components are assessed against checks; teams earn Certifications by passing all checks at each level. Creates structured improvement pathways visible directly in the Backstage UI.

## Integration

Operates within the Backstage ecosystem, surfacing health metrics alongside the Software Catalog. Enables organizations to define development and operational standards and track compliance across all services.
