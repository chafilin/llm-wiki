# GitLab — CI/CD Analytics

**Source:** https://docs.gitlab.com/user/analytics/ci_cd_analytics/

## Overview

Pipeline performance insights. Navigate via **Analyze > CI/CD analytics**.

## Key Metrics

1. Total pipeline runs (including child pipelines)
2. Median duration
3. Failure rate
4. Success rate
5. Other rate (skipped/canceled)

## Filtering

Filter by source, branch, or custom date ranges.

## Visualization Charts

**Pipeline Duration Chart** — median (P50) and P95 execution times.

**Pipeline Status Chart** — distribution of successful, failed, and other-status pipelines over time.

## Advanced Job Metrics (Premium/Ultimate)

Per-job statistics: P50 duration, P95 duration, failure rates. Sortable by mean duration. Shows 10 jobs/page with search.
