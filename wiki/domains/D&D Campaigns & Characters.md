## Characters

### Иммераль Брайтвуд (Strada campaign)
*Source: [[Strada character]]*
- Half-elf, 25 years old
- Twilight Cleric of Selûne
- Appearance: red hair (partly braided), green eyes, sharp features, pointed ears
- Background: raised in Waterdeep by his human father, a cleric of Selûne; mother is an elf from a hidden village in the Moonwood. Selûne granted him divine power beyond his parents.
- Currently: plagued by nightmares of shadowy lands; drawn toward Daggerford seeking answers
- Character sheet: [[Strada character]]

### Нир Кано (Pirates campaign)
*Source: [[Пираты]]*
- 30–35 years old, light hair (some braided), brown eyes, simple clothes, many scars (former pirate)
- Lives in the pirate settlement Мёртвая Гавань (Dead Harbor) on a sandbar near Онукеа
- Occupation: repairs sea salvage, builds strange mechanisms, identifies magical artifacts
- Has a free infusion: Lantern of Detection (cannot be replaced)
- Setting: a festival is upcoming; Captain Roger Waike has arrived to celebrate a bold raid
