## Definition
The tendency to stay quiet about not understanding something because admitting confusion feels like a signal of incompetence. Acronym from The Grug Brained Developer.

## Why it matters
FOLD is one of [[Complexity]]'s main power sources. If nobody says "this is too complex", complexity never gets challenged. Over-engineered systems survive because everyone privately thinks they're the only one who doesn't get it.

## The senior dev move
Senior developers publicly saying "this is too complex for me" is one of the highest-leverage things they can do. It:
- Gives juniors permission to admit the same
- Reframes "I don't understand this" from a personal failure to a system design problem
- Puts pressure on the author to simplify rather than on the reader to heroically comprehend

## In practice
- When reviewing code: say "I don't understand what this does" rather than approving and hoping
- In design discussions: "this feels too complex" is a legitimate engineering objection, not an opinion
- The snide remark from the "big brain" who defends complexity is a status move, not a technical argument
