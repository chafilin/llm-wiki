## Overview
Frontend developer at Manychat, based in Barcelona. Works at the intersection of frontend quality, tooling, and dependency maintenance. Relocated to Spain in 2025 — that transition took a significant mental load.

## Strengths
- Prefers solid, long-term fixes over quick patches — good for quality
- Improved collaboration over time: asks questions faster, builds connections across teams
- Comfortable driving cross-team technical initiatives (e.g. organized React update tests across frontend teams, spoke at internal meetup)
- Good at technical investigation and cleanup (security audits, dependency upgrades, Highcharts removal, endpoint refactoring)

## Recurring patterns to watch
- **Optimistic estimations** — consistently underestimates timelines; needs to add buffer deliberately, especially for uncommon project types
- **Stubbornness on technical problems** — tendency to keep "beating" an issue past the point where stopping is right. The right level of solution matters.
- **Delayed visibility** — shows work too late; documentation PR was polished too long before sharing. Show progress earlier, even when incomplete.
- **Recovery time underestimated** — the Barcelona move was draining; learned to plan buffer and actually take vacation

## What I want to change
- Ask for help earlier on environment/tooling rabbit holes
- Communicate ETA or scope changes within 24h of discovering them
- Estimate uncommon projects more conservatively
