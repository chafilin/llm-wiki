## Summary
Trivago's 2015 account of moving from "trunk is broken" to a stable quality culture. The key finding: tooling and process changes (CI, Git, standardized environments) were necessary but insufficient — the real lever was team-based ownership and cultural change.

## Key takeaways
- Pre-CI state: push to trunk without running tests; staging used as test environment
- Process fixes (code freezes, QA resources, release managers) created stress without improving stability
- What actually worked: CI + Git + Vagrant + acceptance testing + SOA architecture
- But more importantly: team ownership, agile processes, feature branches with peer review, guilds
- "Tooling and processes will continue to change, but the culture is here to stay"
- Tension: startup speed vs. sustainable quality — neither wins completely, balance must be conscious
