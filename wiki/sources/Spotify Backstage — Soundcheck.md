## Summary
Soundcheck is a Backstage plugin for measuring and improving software health. Organizations define Checks (standards), group them into Tracks with progressive Levels, and earn Certifications when all checks at a level pass.

## Key takeaways
- Five concepts: Check (measurable standard), Check Result (pass/fail), Track (health initiative), Level (milestone grouping of checks), Certification (earned by passing all checks at a level)
- Creates structured improvement pathways visible alongside the Backstage software catalog
- Incentivizes quality and reliability adoption through certification gamification
