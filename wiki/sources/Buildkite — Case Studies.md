## Summary
Collection of Buildkite customer results across Reddit, PagerDuty, Intercom, Elastic, Shopify, and REA Group. Shows real-world CI/CD improvements with specific metrics.

## Key takeaways
- Reddit: 30% faster builds, queue times from minutes → 5 seconds, checkout from minutes → 30-40 sec
- Intercom: 25 min → 3 min (85%), 150 daily deployments
- Elastic (Kibana): 3 hours → 55 min (70%), cloud costs -75%
- Shopify: <5 min builds, 8,000 pipelines, 300M jobs/year; 300% team growth maintained
- REA Group: team setup weeks → days (80% faster), 120,000 builds/month
- PagerDuty: 1M builds (Jan–Oct 2023), 20% faster incident resolution
