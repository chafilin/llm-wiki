## Summary
Atlassian's multi-year DX transformation story as told in LinearB's blog. Starting point: dev satisfaction below 50%. Key interventions: 10% principle (budgeted productivity time per team), Champions Program, Compass internal developer portal.

## Key takeaways
- Starting problem: dev satisfaction below 50%; three friction points: difficult processes, perceived lack of autonomy, fear of breaking complex systems
- Two-strategy framework: Champions Program (cross-functional, drives org-wide investments) + 10% Principle (each team gets 10% capacity for productivity, tracked in Jira)
- Metrics: moved beyond PR cycle time to issue cycle time, self-service doc effectiveness, dependency ease, monthly dev satisfaction surveys
- Results: one team 48-hour prod deployment rate 12% → 32% in one quarter; another 300% improvement in deployment frequency
- Compass: internal developer portal → component catalog + health scorecards + templates + extensibility
- "Developer joy unlocks sustained productivity"
