## Summary
Bazel community page listing organizations using Bazel. Adoption spans major tech companies (Google, Uber, Dropbox, Twitter, Stripe) and many mid-size companies achieving 5–10× CI speed improvements via remote caching.

## Key takeaways
- Google: all Google software, designed for millions of daily builds
- Notable improvements: Tokopedia 55→5 min iOS (remote caching), Canva 5-6× decrease, Redfin 40-90→5-6 min
- Uber: one of the largest Go monorepos, 900+ developers
- Open source projects: TensorFlow, Angular, Envoy, gRPC, Selenium, and 50+ others
