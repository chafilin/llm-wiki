## Summary
An example GitLab merge request auto-generated by the `gitlab-housekeeper` bot to quarantine a flaky feature spec. Illustrates GitLab's fully automated identification-to-MR pipeline — humans only decide what to do once a test is already flagged.

## Key takeaways
- Bot (`gitlab-housekeeper` with `Keeps::QuarantineFlakyTests`) auto-creates MR when test meets flakiness thresholds
- Criteria: "flakiness::1"/"flakiness::2" (failure at/above 95th percentile) OR "severity::1" (daily spikes exceed threshold)
- Teams can accept quarantine or close MR in favor of deleting the test
- Automation removes friction from "I should quarantine this but haven't gotten to it"
- Engineering Productivity issue tracker tracks feedback and decisions
