## Summary
GitLab CI/CD Analytics documentation. Provides pipeline duration (P50/P95), failure/success rates, and per-job statistics (Premium/Ultimate). The measurement foundation for CI optimization work.

## Key takeaways
- Key metrics: total runs (including child pipelines), median duration, failure rate, success rate
- Charts: Pipeline Duration (P50 and P95) and Pipeline Status (distribution over time)
- Per-job stats (Premium/Ultimate): P50 duration, P95 duration, failure rates, sortable by mean duration
- Filter by source, branch, custom date ranges
- Accessed via Analyze > CI/CD analytics
